# Physical Robot Hardware Specifications

## 1. Ranger Mini v2 Mobile Base
- Omnidirectional steering drive with 4x steering servos and 4x BLDC hub motors.
- 24V 30Ah LiFePO4 battery pack with inline safety contactor.
- Max payload: 50 kg | Max speed: 1.5 m/s.

## 2. Piper 6-DOF Manipulator
- 6 revolute joints + parallel linear gripper.
- Rated payload: 1.5 kg | Max reach: 626 mm.
- Operating bus: CAN 2.0B @ 1 Mbps (`can1`).
